# NEXT Support Navigator

## Product guardrails

- NEXT supports human agency. It presents possible paths, relevant support, and a possible next step; it must never make decisions for a person.
- Treat a person's story as sensitive information. Do not log it or expose it in client-side analytics.
- Keep the MVP focused on the guided journey: situation, goal, strengths, barriers, support, and one next step.

## Development workflow

- Build and verify one focused feature at a time.
- Keep the interface welcoming, calm, accessible, and usable on small screens.
- Update `HANDOFF.md` when architecture, setup, or the next development task changes.
- Do not add authentication, persistence, AI integrations, or external services until their product contract is defined.
